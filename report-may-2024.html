Monthly Letter · No. 03

May 2024 Investment Letter

Political continuity was increasingly priced in through May; sector selection and risk discipline mattered more than short-term noise, with FMCG, PSU banks and IT flagged as favourable risk-reward.

May 2024Vesara Research5 min readMonthly Letter
In brief
  • Markets priced in continuity of the incumbent government; elections are framed as a game of preparation and strategy, best approached without emotional attachment.
  • PSU banks, FMCG and IT offer favourable risk-reward; CPSU, PSSE, metals, manufacturing, auto and MNCs lead. A simultaneous rise in the dollar, gold and 10-year yields reads as consolidation, not an outright bearish signal.

May 2024 was a favourable month, driven by strategic positioning in key sectors and effective risk management. The market's pricing-in of political continuity, combined with a discipline of ignoring short-term noise, played a significant role. The focus ahead stays on sectors and stocks with favourable risk-reward, guided by broad market analysis and long-term trends.

Market Analysis

May was notably influenced by political dynamics, with the general consensus pricing in continuity of the current government. Observations suggested the opposition made some strategic errors and lacked thorough preparation, further bolstering market confidence. Elections — often a blend of strategy and spectacle — underscore the importance of not becoming emotionally invested in any one side, but rather focusing on objective analysis.

Election Impact

Elections significantly affect sentiment, acting as a game in which preparation and strategy determine the outcome. The political climate, characterised by strategic missteps from the opposition, reinforced a positive market outlook on the incumbent government. This environment presents both challenges and opportunities and demands a balanced approach.

Sector Analysis and Recommendations

Sectors with favourable risk-reward. PSU Banks, FMCG and IT offer a promising risk-reward profile. Within FMCG, specific stocks to watch include VBL, UBL, Britannia, Godrej Consumer Products, ITC and Marico; the upcoming World Cup and increased government spending in rural areas support the bullish case for FMCG.

Strongest sectors. CPSU, PSSE, metals, India manufacturing, auto and MNCs are currently leading the market, exhibiting strong performance and likely to continue their upward trajectory.

Ignoring short-term noise. It is crucial not to be swayed by short-term fluctuations and speculative noise; maintaining focus on long-term trends and fundamentals leads to more stable and predictable outcomes.

Risk-reward in the market. In the short term the market does not offer a compelling risk-reward scenario; however, strategic positioning in key sectors can mitigate this and provide steadier returns.

Impact of a Rising Dollar Index, Gold and 10-Year Yields

A concurrent rise in the dollar index, gold and 10-year yields typically signals potential pressure on equity markets. This is not necessarily bearish over the long term; it can instead represent a consolidation phase, creating opportunities for equities to stabilise and potentially resume their uptrend. Watching these asset classes offers insight into sentiment and future moves.

Conclusion

May 2024 was a favourable month, achieved through strategic sector positioning, effective risk management, and a focus on long-term trends over short-term noise. The approach ahead continues to leverage comprehensive market analysis and disciplined execution to capture sustainable growth opportunities.
