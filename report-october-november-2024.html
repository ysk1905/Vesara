Introduction

In October and November 2024, the portfolio delivered steady results, achieving returns of 3.2% and 0.8%, respectively. These gains were achieved despite significant market volatility and corrections in midcap, small cap, and microcap indices. Throughout this period, the strategy adopted a cautious approach, staying mostly in cash in November due to continuous market uncertainty and focusing primarily on long-term investments to safeguard portfolio stability.

Market Overview

Correction Trends: The broader market went through a natural cooling-off period, with large-cap indices initially resisting the correction but eventually succumbing to downward trends as FIIs pulled out funds. Midcap and small cap indices underperformed, but recent consolidation suggests potential opportunities for recovery. Global and Domestic Influences: Global Market Breadth: Approximately 78% of S&P 500 stocks remain above their 200 DMA, reflecting strong global market structure despite volatility.

Indian Market Breadth: Only 40% of Indian stocks are above their 50 DMA, signaling short-term weakness. However, 71% above the 200 DMA indicates that long-term trends remain bullish. Sectoral Insights: Strong Sectors: Banks, Metals, and IT: These sectors exhibited strong relative strength, with successful retests of key technical levels like the 50 DMA.

They remain potential leaders for the next market rally. Healthcare and Private Banks: Resilient during corrections, these sectors are well-positioned to take leadership roles in the next market cycle. Underperforming Sectors: Oil and Gas, FMCG, and Defense: These sectors experienced corrections due to overvaluation but retain importance for long-term stability.

Conclusion

October and November highlighted the importance of maintaining a disciplined and cautious approach during uncertain market conditions. Staying mostly in cash during November allowed for risk mitigation while focusing on long-term investment opportunities. As the portfolio look ahead, the outlook remains focused on gradual exposure, monitoring breadth indicators, and aligning with high-potential sectors such as banks, metals, IT, and healthcare to capitalize on emerging opportunities.
